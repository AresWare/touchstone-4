A love for the everyday loaf

About North Star Bakery

Rooted in the simple pleasure of sharing good bread.

Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery concept built around artisan bread, pastries, and a friendly welcome. The idea is simple: make everyday moments feel a little more special, whether you are sharing a loaf with friends or picking up something sweet.

Our name, North Star, reflects our guiding principle: to be a steady, reliable presence in our community, always pointing toward quality and craftsmanship. Just as the North Star has guided travelers for centuries, we aim to guide our customers toward the simple joy of truly good bread.

Our Philosophy

We believe in the pleasure of a well-made loaf and the care that goes into it. Our website celebrates that craft with a close look at rustic crusts, hand-shaped breads, and treats to share.

Our Bakers

Our small team of bakers arrives before dawn each day to mix, shape, and bake. They bring years of experience and a genuine love for the craft. When you visit, you'll often see them working behind the counter, happy to share recommendations or talk about the bread-making process.

The Craft

The art of bread-making is a balance of science and intuition. Our bakers read the dough by feel, adjusting hydration and fermentation times based on temperature, humidity, and the character of the starter on any given day. It's a craft that takes years to master and a lifetime to perfect.
